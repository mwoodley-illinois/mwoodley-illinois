I'm an Assistant Clinical Professor of Computer Science at the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign, where I focus on project-based software engineering courses that emphasize teamwork, iteration, and professional development. I design experiences that help students build real-world skills: working with version control, practicing effective code review, managing scope, and learning to communicate as engineers.

Before entering academia, I spent over two decades in the software industry, leading technical teams and building complex systems — including early work on Microsoft Flight Simulator with subLOGIC and combat flight simulations at Mission Studios. I’ve developed custom CAD tools, reverse-engineered proprietary formats, and always enjoyed the challenge of building what others said couldn't be done.

Today, I’m most interested in helping students develop the habits and mindsets that prepare them for professional environments — from writing clean, testable code to contributing meaningfully on a collaborative team. I also advise course staff and mentors to grow their own engineering and leadership skills through hands-on involvement with student teams.

Outside of teaching and software, I’m an avid woodworker and a lifelong music lover, always in search of a good design challenge or a better sound system.
